# Use of AI-assisted tools

The authors specified the mathematical models, numerical experiments,
acceptance criteria and scientific interpretations, approved every production
campaign, and personally submitted all cluster jobs.

Software for the project was generated with ChatGPT (Astra) from the authors'
specifications and was subsequently audited, tested, refactored and extended
with OpenAI Codex (based on GPT-6). Claude (Opus 5.5) was used in manuscript
text development and review. AI assistance included repository organisation,
implementation and test scaffolding, scheduler templates, provenance checks,
post-processing, figure and table generation, and reproducibility packaging.

The scale of the work included 52 declared training configurations, 32 frozen
downstream checkpoints, thousands of forward/derivative evaluations, hundreds
of inversion and diagnostic trajectories, and a multi-tier paper-product
pipeline. AI tools therefore performed a role comparable to substantial
research-software-engineering support, a role not otherwise represented among
the authors. They did not choose the scientific questions, run autonomous
external jobs, or replace author review of numerical and mathematical claims.

All released numerical products are tied to machine-readable inputs and
checksums. The authors remain responsible for the code and manuscript.
