# Analyses reported in the paper

The public paper pipeline includes:

1. independent headline `E_F`, `E_J` and combined test error;
2. T1/T2 noiseless headline inversion;
3. derivative-informed versus forward-only models;
4. common-basepoint JVP, ambient VJP and preconditioned VJP diagnostics;
5. identity and Matérn length-scale controls, including exact FEM;
6. FNO capacity and training-size semiconvergence studies;
7. low-smoothness Matérn geometries and trajectory hypotheses;
8. T1/T2 noisy inversions and retrospective raw-noise stopping; and
9. held-out surrogate-error thresholds and offline cost accounting.

The exact sources for every displayed and prose-only result are listed in the
private release `PAPER_MAP.md` and encoded in the Tier-A bundle manifest.
